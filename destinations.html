PLACES TO EXPLORE

Choose your next adventure.

Here are three types of destinations that can make a trip memorable.

01 / THE COAST

Beach towns

Spend the day by the water, try local seafood, and enjoy a slower pace of life.

02 / THE MOUNTAINS

Mountain villages

Discover scenic trails, cool weather, and peaceful landscapes far from busy streets.

03 / THE CITY

Historic cities

Walk through old neighborhoods, visit museums, and taste dishes loved by local people.

A simple travel tip

Before you leave, check the weather, local transport, opening hours, and any entry requirements. A little planning can make your trip much easier.
